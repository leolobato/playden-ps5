Sign in to GOGPlayden on PS5

Open this page by scanning the code on the PS5.

  1. Sign in to your GOG account.

    Open GOG sign-in

  2. Copy the address of the page that opens after you sign in.

    It is the page that opens after you sign in, starting with embed.gog.com. The page itself can look empty.

  3. Come back here, paste the address and choose Send.
